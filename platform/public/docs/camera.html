Camera

A Camera sets which part of the scene is on screen. Without one, the scene's (0, 0) is the top-left corner. Cameras don't move UI.

#Following

Put the Camera inside the player, or set its target (or call Camera:follow(piece)). smoothing glides (0 sticks); offsetX and offsetY shift the view; snap() jumps to the target.

Camera.offsetX = approach(Camera.offsetX, sign(vx) * 60, 120 * dt)

#Limits

left, top, right and bottom stop the view at those edges. setLimits(l, t, r, b) sets all four; limitTo(tiles) uses a Tiles piece's painted area. A limited area smaller than the screen is centered.

#Zoom and shake

zoom = 2 doubles the size. shake(strength, seconds): shake(6, 0.3).

#Several cameras

The first active camera is used. makeCurrent() switches; camera() returns the one in use.

#Coordinates
mouse()The pointer in the scene
Camera:screenToWorld(x, y), worldToScreen(x, y)Convert between screen and scene
Camera:getView()The visible area: x, y, width, height
 
local vx, vy, vw, vh = camera():getView()
spawn("scenes/bat.scene", { x = vx + vw + 32, y = vy + rnd(vh) })
PropertyTypeDefault
activebooltrueUse this camera. When several are active, the first in the scene wins.
zoomnumber12 shows everything twice as big.
smoothingnumber0How gently it catches up: 0 sticks exactly, 5 glides.
targetpiecepath""A piece to follow, wherever the camera is in the scene (empty: the camera's own position).
offsetXnumber (px)0Looks this far right of what it follows.
offsetYnumber (px)0Looks this far below what it follows.
leftnumber (px)noneDon't show anything left of this (empty for no edge).
topnumber (px)noneDon't show anything above this.
rightnumber (px)noneDon't show anything right of this.
bottomnumber (px)noneDon't show anything below this.
Method
shake(strength?, seconds?)Shakes the view: shake(6, 0.3).
follow(piece)Follows a piece (nil: back to target, or the camera's own position).
snap()Jumps straight to what it follows, without smoothing (after a teleport).
setLimits(left, top, right, bottom)Sets the edges it doesn't show past (nil for no edge).
limitTo(piece)Keeps the view inside the painted part of a Tiles piece.
makeCurrent()Makes this the camera in use, turning the others off.
getView()The part of the scene on screen: x, y, width, height.
screenToWorld(sx, sy)A point on the screen, as a point in the scene.
worldToScreen(x, y)A point in the scene, as a point on the screen.
