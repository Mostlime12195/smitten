Physics

Each scene has a Box2D 3 world. Bodies are solid, Zones detect overlaps, Hitboxes add shapes, Joints connect bodies, and solid tiles are walls and floors. Physics steps 60 times a second, after physicsUpdate(dt).

Units: pixels, pixels per second, radians (clockwise), kilograms. y points down.

#Bodies

motionMoved byFor
stillNothing, unless a script moves itFloors, walls, moving platforms
scriptedvx, vy and move()Players, enemies
physicsGravity, collisions, forces, jointsCrates, balls, debris

motion can change at any time.

shape
autoFits the Sprites and Shapes inside
rectanglewidth × height
circleradius
capsulewidth × height, round ends
polygonpoints {x1, y1, x2, y2, ...}, up to 8, convex
noneHitboxes only

Shapes move, turn and scale with the piece. A Hitbox inside a Body or Zone adds a shape; off = true disables it.

#Scripted bodies

function physicsUpdate(dt)
  vx = axis("left", "right") * 220
  vy = math.min(vy + 1800 * dt, 1000)
  if pressed("jump") and onGround() then vy = -jumpSpeed(96) end
  move()
end

move() moves by vx * dt, vy * dt. It slides along surfaces, climbs slopes up to slope (45°), stays grounded over small steps, rides moving platforms, pushes physics bodies according to mass, passes up through one-way tiles, and turns by spin.
onGround(), onWall(), onCeiling()What the last move touched
moveBy(dx, dy)Moves until blocked; returns what it hit, or nil

Setting x, y or rotation moves the body there, pushing what's in the way for short distances.

#Physics bodies

vx, vy and spin are the current velocity; set them to throw.
applyImpulse(ix, iy[, x, y])Instant change of velocity by impulse / mass; at a point, also spins
applyForce(fx, fy[, x, y])Force for one step; call every physicsUpdate
applyTorque(t), applyAngularImpulse(i)Turning force; instant turn
wake()Wakes a sleeping body
grab(x, y), dragTo(x, y), drop()Hold, move and release by a point; keeps momentum
Setting
massKilograms
gravityGravity scale: 0 floats, -1 falls up
friction, bounce0 to 1
drag, spinDragLinear and angular damping
fixedRotationNever turns
bulletContinuous collision against other moving bodies
asleepStart at rest

Setting x, y or rotation teleports and keeps velocity.

for _, p in ipairs(overlap("circle", x, y, 150)) do
  if p.motion == "physics" then
    local dx, dy = p.x - x, p.y - y
    local d = math.max(dist(0, 0, dx, dy), 1)
    p:applyImpulse(dx / d * 600 * p.mass, dy / d * 600 * p.mass)
  end
end

#Collisions

hit(other, contact) runs on both bodies when they start touching.

contact
x, yContact point
nx, nyNormal, from other towards this body
impulseStrength of the impact
isTouching(pieceOrTag)Whether it touches it now
touching(pieceOrTag)The first touching match, or nil
contacts()Current contacts: {piece, x, y, nx, ny, impulse}
touchingTile(setting)Settings of a touching tile that has setting

passThrough lists tags this body ignores, both ways; "tiles" ignores solid tiles.

#Zones

entered(other) and exited(other) run on the Zone and on the Body. detects limits them to tags, active = false turns the Zone off, and inside() lists what's in it. Zones don't block and don't detect still bodies.

#Tiles

Solid tiles have smooth seams. oneWay tiles block only from above. Changes by setTile, fill and clear apply immediately. See Tiles.

#Queries

Global in scripts and on smitten.physics:

Call
raycast(x1, y1, x2, y2, tags)The first body or solid tiles on a line: { piece, x, y, nx, ny, fraction }, or nil. tags (optional) only counts pieces with one of them.
raycastAll(x1, y1, x2, y2, tags)Everything on a line, nearest first: a list of { piece, x, y, nx, ny, fraction }.
piecesAt(x, y, tags)The bodies and zones whose shapes contain a point.
overlap(kind, x, y, ...)The bodies and zones touching a shape: overlap("circle", x, y, radius) or overlap("rect", x, y, width, height).
getGravity()The scene's gravity: x, y in pixels per second per second.
setGravity(x, y)Changes the scene's gravity: setGravity(0, 0) floats everything.
 
local hit = raycast(x, y, player.x, player.y)
local sees = hit and hit.piece == player

Raycasts ignore Zones and the backs of tiles.

#Gravity

Set in Project settings › Physics (physics.gravity, default [0, 980]). setGravity(x, y) and getGravity() change it at run time. Scripted bodies apply their own gravity.

#Joints

A Joint connects body a to body b, or to its own position when b is empty. It acts at the Joint's position.

kind
pinRotates around the Joint. motor, limit (minAngle, maxAngle), spring
weldFixed together; spring makes it flex
ropeAt most length apart
springPulls to length: stiffness, damping
distanceExactly length apart
sliderMoves along the Joint's x axis. motor, limit (lower, upper), spring
wheelRotates, and springs along the Joint's y axis. motor
mousePulls a towards the Joint's position

Settings apply while running. collide lets connected bodies collide. breakForce above 0 breaks the joint under that force and emits broke. Read state with getAngle(), getTranslation(), getLength(), getSpeed(), getForce() and getTorque().

#love.physics

LÖVE's love.physics (Box2D 2.4) works as in LÖVE, as the optional lovephysics module. Scenes don't use it.
